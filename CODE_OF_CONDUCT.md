# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FreeTimers a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, accountability, and focus on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing private
information, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the
contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

## Resumen en español

La comunidad debe ser abierta, respetuosa e inclusiva. El acoso, ataques
personales y publicación de información privada no se aceptan. Los incidentes
se reportan de forma privada al propietario del repositorio.
